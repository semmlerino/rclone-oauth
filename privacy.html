Privacy policy

"rclone restic backup" is a personal installation of rclone, used only by its owner with their own Google account.

  • It reads and writes files in the owner's Google Drive only to store and restore the owner's backups.
  • Backups are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload.
  • No data is collected, sold, or shared with anyone, and no other users' data is accessed.
  • Access tokens stay on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
